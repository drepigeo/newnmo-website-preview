स्वास्थ्य सेवा, राष्ट्र सेवा — Swasthya Sewa, Rashtra Sewa

The National Medicos Organisation (NMO) is an all-India fraternity of qualified allopathic doctors, dental surgeons, faculties, and medical students. Conceived by medical student Dr. Dhanakar Thakur in 1977, NMO bridges healthcare disparities across tribal, rural, and border frontiers of Bharat with selfless medical service, academic excellence, and national character.

Genesis & Constitutional Charter

About National Medicos Organisation

Conceived on August 8, 1977, by 4th-year DMCH medical student Dr. Dhanakar Thakur at Village Samaul and formally founded on November 5, 1977, in Varanasi, NMO unites healthcare professionals in the sacred task of national reconstruction.

The Ethos of Swasthya Sewa, Rashtra Sewa

Recognizing that modern healthcare in India often concentrated heavily in urban medical centers while leaving rural and tribal populations underserved, NMO was founded to build a bridge of compassionate service and ethical professionalism. Registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Registration No. 21/1987-88), NMO operates campus chapters and professional branches across all states of Bharat.

The Four Pillars: Core Aims & Objectives

1

Healthcare Service (स्वास्थ्य सेवा)

Direct clinical delivery to the most disadvantaged, rural, and tribal communities across Bharat. Operating free medical screening camps, disaster emergency relief missions, and longitudinal village and urban slum adoptions to ensure continuity of care and dignity for every patient.

2

Medical Education (आयुर्विज्ञान शिक्षा)

Elevating the academic atmosphere inside medical and dental colleges. Fostering healthy teacher-student relationships, conducting Continuing Medical Education (CME) seminars, and advocating for medical education and textbooks in Indian languages alongside uniform NEET counselling standards.

3

Professional Welfare (चिकित्सक कल्याण)

Safeguarding the workplace safety, dignity, and career aspirations of doctors, residents, and medicos. Conducting nationwide surveys on DNB and residency training, mentoring young graduates, addressing mental health, and spearheading suicide prevention campaigns among medical students.

4

Social Responsibility (राष्ट्र सेवा व सामाजिक चेतना)

Inculcating patriotic character, moral discipline, and cultural consciousness (Bhartiya Sanskruti) among healthcare providers. Channeling the energy of medical youth into national reconstruction, de-commercializing medicine, and reviving the ethical spirit of the Charaka Shapath.

Founder, National Medicos Organisation • MBBS, MD (Gen. Med), DCH (DMCH)

Documented Milestones & Evolution

History & Legacy (1977 – 2026)

A documented chronological journey distinguishing foundational historical events from continuing contemporary programs.

Phase I: Documented Historical Milestones (1977 – 2003) Foundational Era
August 8, 1977

Genesis at Village Samaul (Madhubani, Bihar)

Dr. Dhanakar Thakur, then a fourth-year medical student at DMCH, conceived the conceptual blueprint of an all-India organisation of medicos and doctors to address rural healthcare neglect and promote national service.

August 14, 1977

Drafting of the Preamble at DMCH Hostel

The foundational Preamble and initial charter were penned at the Darbhanga Medical College Boys' Hostel, articulating the commitment of medical youth to rural service, national character, and ethical practice.

August 25 & October 3, 1977

Preliminary Consultations in Mumbai & Darbhanga

Dr. Thakur held consultative discussions with medical professionals in Mumbai, followed by a formal medicos gathering during the ABVP Bihar State Conference at Darbhanga uniting delegates from Patna, Darbhanga, and Muzaffarpur.

November 5, 1977

Historic Foundation Assembly at Varanasi

Approximately 40 medical students, interns, and senior teachers from across India assembled at Varanasi to formally inaugurate the National Medicos Organisation, establishing November 5 as NMO Foundation Day.

January 1978

Adoption of Mankind Prayer & Sacred Insignia

Adoption of King Rantideva's prayer (न त्वहं कामये राज्यं...) as NMO's mankind mantra and creation of the official seal depicting Lord Dhanvantari over the map of Bharat with the motto प्राणिनाम् आर्तिनाशनम्.

April 23, 1981

Launch of Aayurvigyan Pragati Journal at Ranchi

Inauguration of NMO's official bilingual journal Aayurvigyan Pragati, founded and edited by Dr. Dhanakar Thakur. The journal went on to publish over 55 issues, championing medical literature in Hindi and Indian languages.

1987 – 1988

Statutory Registration under Societies Registration Act

Formally registered under the Societies Registration Act, 1860 (Registration No. 21/1987-88), with its founding registered office at Leheriasarai, Bihar, cementing its status as an all-India public-service society.

March 31, 1988

Bhagalpur National Conference & Tribal Sickle Cell Study

National Conference focused on endemic sickle-cell disease and hemoglobinopathies among tribal populations of the Santhal Parganas, pioneering field epidemiological surveys by medical students.

1989

Gwalior Conference & Adoption of the Charaka Shapath

Historic resolution advocating the revival of the ancient Indian medical oath (Charaka Shapath) as a moral code for graduating physicians, emphasizing de-commercialization and compassionate care.

2003

Inception of the Flagship Dhanvantari Sewa Yatra

Launch of the annual mega-medical mission across the North Eastern frontier states (Assam, Arunachal Pradesh, Manipur, Meghalaya, Nagaland, Tripura, Mizoram), serving remote border villages lacking primary healthcare.

Phase II: Contemporary Era & Continuing Programmes (2014 – Present) Active Era
2014

Rishi Kashyap Swasthya Sewa Yatra (RKSSY) Launch

Following catastrophic floods in Jammu & Kashmir in 2014, NMO mobilized 68 doctors and initiated the annual RKSSY mission providing free multi-specialty care in remote mountain villages of Jammu, Kashmir, and Ladakh.

2020 – 2021

Pan-India COVID-19 Telemedicine Care & Blood Drives

Mobilized over 11,000 free telemedicine consultations during lockdowns, distributed medical kits to underserved communities, and coordinated institutional voluntary blood and plasma donation drives nationwide.

2024

NMOCON at AIIMS Rishikesh & National Health Day Focus

Organized National Conference at AIIMS Rishikesh under the theme Our Health • Our Nature • Our Culture. Celebrated National Health Day (Nov 5) with a nationwide campaign addressing mental health and suicide prevention among medicos.

2025 – 2026

DOAJ Indexing of The NMO Journal & NMOCON Gujarat

The NMO Journal achieved international indexing in the Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ) with National Medical Commission (NMC) recognition. Planning for NMOCON 2026 in Gujarat.

Annual 7-day medical mission conducted across remote high-altitude regions of Jammu, Kashmir, and Ladakh with balanced doctor-to-student teams providing diagnostic and pharmaceutical support.

Periodic Community Drives • Urban & Semi-Urban
Current Programme

24/7 Registry • Collegiate Chapters

Continuous • State Chapters & Hospitals

Medical Education & Publications

Documenting authentic academic literature, policy resolutions, and editorial contributions from NMO's founding to the modern era.

Founded 1981 • 55+ Issues

Aayurvigyan Pragati (आयुर्विज्ञान प्रगति)

Pioneering medical and social journal of NMO, founded on April 23, 1981, at Ranchi by Dr. Dhanakar Thakur. Serving as Chief Editor, Dr. Thakur published over 55 issues championing medical writing in Hindi and Indian languages, rural clinical case studies, and medical humanities.

Founder Editor: Dr. Dhanakar Thakur • Darbhanga / Ranchi
DOAJ Indexed • NMC Recognized

The NMO Journal

The contemporary official peer-reviewed scientific journal of National Medicos Organisation. Publishing original research articles, public health audits, and clinical reviews, it has earned formal recognition from the National Medical Commission (NMC) and indexing in DOAJ.

Index Status: Directory of Open Access Journals (DOAJ)
Policy Submissions & Whitepapers

Medical Education Reform Resolutions

Formal policy whitepapers submitted to the National Medical Commission and Ministry of Health, including recommendations on introducing medical textbooks in Indian languages, implementing uniform NEET counselling, and establishing fair residency norms.

DNB Institute Surveys & Resident Mentorship Repositories

Comprehensive surveys compiled by NMO members to provide transparent, unbiased guidance to post-graduate aspirants regarding clinical exposure, academics, and stipends across accredited DNB hospitals.
